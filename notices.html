CHECK BEFORE YOU ACT

Verify notice applicability

Read the complete official notice and verify the following details before relying on it:

  • Notice number and issue date.
  • Programme, semester and academic year.
  • Admission batch and applicable autonomous regulations.
  • Action required, responsible person/office and deadline, where specified.
  • Any corrigendum or superseding notice issued subsequently.

Check the admission batch and semester carefully, as autonomous regulations may apply progressively to specific cohorts.

Need clarification?

For clarification about a published examination notice, contact the Examination Section through the official institutional communication channel. Quote the notice number and date in your communication.
