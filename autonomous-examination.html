ACADEMIC AUTONOMY

Autonomous Examination Framework

Overview of the examination and assessment framework for programmes and batches operating under academic autonomy at ADYPSOE.

Applicability

Progressive implementation of autonomy

ADYPSOE is implementing academic autonomy progressively. The autonomous examination framework applies only to the programmes, semesters and admission batches formally brought under autonomy. Students and faculty must verify the applicability stated in the relevant academic regulations, academic calendar and examination notifications.

Important: Do not assume that an autonomous regulation or examination schedule applies to a batch that continues under the SPPU-affiliated scheme.

40 MARKS · CONTINUOUS INTERNAL EVALUATION

Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE)

CIE is divided into two parts: CIE-I (MidSem Examinations) and CIE-II (CCE Assessments). Together, these two components contribute 40 marks to the CIE total.

END-SEMESTER ASSESSMENT

Semester End Examination (SEE)

The Semester End Examination is conducted according to the approved autonomous examination scheme. The official examination information for each SEE session should identify the programme, semester, academic year, examination session, subject/course codes, timetable, examination instructions and applicable regulations.

  • SEE timetable and any revised timetable.
  • Examination form and hall-ticket instructions, where applicable.
  • Examination-day rules and permitted materials.
  • Evaluation, result declaration and post-result processes.

Actual marks distribution, examination duration, question paper pattern and passing requirements will be stated only with reference to the applicable approved regulations.

Examination governance

The autonomous examination system is supported by institutional examination governance and designated committees. The portal is intended to provide the approved scope, procedures and public-facing information for these mechanisms.

  • Board of Examinations (BoE): examination-related governance and oversight as prescribed by the applicable institutional framework.
  • Examination Complaint Redressal Committee: examination-related complaint and grievance redressal channel.
  • Lapses & Malpractice Committee: matters relating to examination lapses and malpractice, as governed by approved rules.
  • Examinations Remuneration Committee: examination-related remuneration processes as per approved institutional provisions.

Committee composition, delegated powers, meeting records and internal deliberations are not public website content unless formally approved for publication.

Examination grievances and malpractice

Students should use the formally notified examination complaint and grievance process. Each notification should specify the submission channel, required particulars, supporting documents and applicable time limit.

Cases involving examination lapses or malpractice must be handled under the approved institutional rules and due process. The public portal may provide the applicable procedure and forms, but must not disclose individual case details or confidential records.

Results and post-result processes

Result notifications, marks-related queries, verification or revaluation processes (where provided under the applicable regulations), application procedures and deadlines will be published through official notices.

DOCUMENT CONTROL

Approved rules are authoritative

This page is an information overview, not a substitute for the approved examination rules and regulations. Where a difference arises, the latest formally approved regulation, ordinance, circular or examination notification applicable to the student's batch will prevail.

Before publishing detailed examination patterns, SEE marks distribution, examination duration, passing criteria, deadlines or procedural rules on this portal, the Examination Section should verify the exact wording and version against the approved institutional documents.
