ACADEMIC AUTONOMY

Autonomous Examination

Information on the examination framework, assessment components, regulations and governance applicable to autonomous batches.

Batch applicability

Progressive implementation of autonomy

Academic autonomy is being implemented progressively. Students and faculty must refer to the approved regulations and examination notices applicable to their admission batch, programme and semester. Do not assume that autonomous regulations apply to every batch.

Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE)

The institutional CIE framework comprises the components notified for the applicable autonomous programme. The CIE structure includes:

  • Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation (CCE): ongoing assessment activities as prescribed in the course and programme framework.
  • Mid-Term Examination: scheduled internal examination as per the approved academic and examination calendar.

Refer to the approved regulations and course-specific instructions for marks distribution, assessment methods and passing requirements.

Semester End Examination (SEE)

SEE schedules, examination pattern, question paper instructions, evaluation and result processes will be published as per the approved regulations for the relevant batch.

Examination governance & committees

Information about the Board of Examinations, examination grievance redressal, lapses and malpractice procedures, remuneration and related governance mechanisms will be provided through approved institutional documents.

Rules, regulations & examination manual

Only the latest approved versions should be treated as authoritative. Superseded drafts and working documents will not be presented as current regulations.
