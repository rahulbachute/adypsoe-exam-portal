OFFICIAL STUDENT APPLICATION

Download the ADYPSOE Examination App

Students are instructed to download the ADYPSOE Examination application from the Google Play Store using the official link below. The app provides convenient access to important examination-related information and student support services.

Download from Google Play ↗

Key features of the Examination App

  • Upcoming examination countdown: View a countdown timer on the app home screen showing the time remaining until the forthcoming examination.
  • Examination seating arrangement: View your examination seating arrangement on the day of the examination through the app.
  • Examination-related grievances: Submit examination-related grievances through the app for the concerned section to review.

The availability of countdown and seating information depends on the examination schedule and seating data being published by the Examination Section. Grievances should include accurate details and relevant information to help the section review the matter.

  1. Open the Google Play Store link on your Android device.
  2. Check that the application is the ADYPSOE Examination app.
  3. Tap Install and wait for installation to complete.
  4. Open the application and follow the instructions issued by the institute or Examination Section.
  5. Keep the application updated so that you have the latest version.

Important: Use only the official Google Play Store link provided here. If you face installation or login difficulties, contact the Examination Section through the officially notified channel. Do not share your password, OTP or other login credentials with anyone.
