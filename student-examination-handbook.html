Student Examination Handbook

A student-friendly guide to assessment, examination eligibility, passing requirements, grades and examination procedures under ADYPSOE Rules and Regulations R-26.

R-26 · Version 1.0

Applicability and important note

This handbook is a concise student guide based on the Institute’s Rules and Regulations for Examination and Evaluation, R-26 / Version 1.0, effective from Academic Year 2026–27. The regulations state that they apply to B.Tech. and M.Tech. students admitted from AY 2026–27.

Autonomy is being implemented progressively. Students must check the applicable programme, admission batch, semester and examination notice before relying on a particular provision. This handbook is a summary; the approved regulations, subsequent amendments and official notices issued by the Institute remain authoritative.

01 · CONTINUOUS INTERNAL EVALUATION

How theory courses are assessed

Theory courses are assessed through Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) and Semester-End Examination (SEE).

Example: 15 out of 20 in MSE becomes 12 out of 16 in CIE. CIE is the combined score of MSE (converted to 16) and CCE (24).

The MSE covers Units 1 and 2. The CCE covers Units 3 to 5. The SEE carries 60 marks and covers Units 1 to 5, with 12 marks allocated to each unit as stated in R-26.

02 · PASSING REQUIREMENTS

Minimum marks required in a theory course

RequirementMinimum
CIE16 out of 40
SEE24 out of 60
Combined CIE + SEE40 out of 100

A student must satisfy all three requirements to pass a theory course. There is no separate passing requirement for MSE and CCE individually; their marks are combined to determine the CIE score.

For Term Work, Practical, Oral, Seminar or Presentation components, the student must secure at least 40% in each prescribed evaluation head, subject to the approved course scheme.

03 · ATTENDANCE AND EXAMINATION ELIGIBILITY

Attendance and course requirements

  • The regulations prescribe a minimum of 75% attendance course-wise, subject to approved condonation provisions.
  • Attendance and other course-completion requirements are considered while determining eligibility for SEE.
  • A student with an attendance or other defined course-completion deficiency may receive the transitional grade FX and may not be permitted to appear for the SEE in that course until the deficiency is resolved through the applicable process.
  • Complete prescribed course requirements, submissions and mandatory activities within the timelines communicated by the department/course coordinator.

05 · RE-SEE AND SUMMER / BACKLOG

What happens if you fail or miss the SEE?

Re-SEE (Semester-End Examination only)

  • Re-SEE is available after declaration of the regular SEE result to students who are otherwise eligible for the course and have failed the SEE (F grade), or have an approved IC arising from absence in the SEE.
  • CIE marks already earned are carried forward. CIE is not re-conducted as part of Re-SEE.
  • A student with FX due to attendance or another course deficiency is not eligible for Re-SEE until the deficiency is resolved through the applicable process.
  • For absence due to a valid reason, the student must submit the prescribed application and supporting documents within the notified time. Approval is by the competent authority.
  • Re-SEE is not a grade-improvement examination. The prescribed examination form and fee requirements apply.

Summer / Backlog Examination

The Institute may conduct a Summer/Backlog Examination for eligible backlog courses, subject to the approved examination schedule, course structure, registration, fee, attempt and eligibility rules. The applicable CIE / Term Work / Practical marks carry-forward provisions are governed by R-26 and the relevant course requirements.

06 · EXAMINATION DAY

Before and during the examination

  • Check the official timetable, examination date, session, venue and any revised instructions published by the Institute.
  • Carry your hall ticket and valid institute identity document as instructed.
  • Report in time and follow the directions of the examination supervisors and invigilators.
  • Use only materials and devices permitted for the particular examination. Unauthorised material, communication or devices are not permitted.
  • Read the question paper and instructions carefully. Write your answers in the prescribed answer book and follow the instructions for additional answer books.
  • Do not leave the examination hall before the permitted time. Follow the announcements and bell instructions issued by the examination staff.
  • Submit the answer book and any required supplementary material before leaving the examination hall.

Any course-specific or examination-specific instructions in the official timetable, hall ticket or circular must also be followed.

07 · WRITER / SCRIBE AND COMPENSATORY TIME

Support for eligible candidates

A candidate requiring a writer/scribe or compensatory time must apply in advance to the Examination Cell in the prescribed format and submit the required documentary evidence. Permission, writer eligibility, verification, separate seating and compensatory time are governed by applicable Government/DEPwD guidelines and the approved Institute policy. Candidates must obtain the required approval and carry the permission letter to the examination.

08 · MALPRACTICE AND EXAMINATION MISCONDUCT

Academic integrity

Cases of unfair means, malpractice or examination-related misconduct are dealt with under the Institute’s approved procedure. The Lapses, Malpractice and Examination Complaint Redressal Committee (LMECRC) examines reported cases, provides an opportunity of being heard and submits findings/recommendations to the competent authority for decision. Students must comply with examination instructions and avoid any prohibited conduct.

09 · RESULTS AND GRIEVANCES

Result queries and answer-book concerns

  • Check the result and grade report through the official channel notified by the Institute.
  • If you notice an error in your grade report, apply to the Controller of Examination through the Programme Head, submitting the original grade report for verification as prescribed in R-26.
  • The regulations provide for an Open House after evaluation of SEE where the examination is conducted in subjective mode on campus. A student with a query regarding SEE assessment may submit it in the prescribed format during the notified Open House.
  • Only candidates appearing for the Open House are permitted to apply for grievances under the stated provision.
  • Revaluation is not permitted for Practical, Oral, Term Work, CIE or other continuously assessed components unless specifically provided otherwise by applicable regulations. Challenges to SEE results are handled under the applicable Institute procedure.

Follow the official notice for the date, process, format and submission deadline. Do not rely on informal requests in place of the prescribed procedure.

10 · PROGRESSION AND DEGREE COMPLETION

Credits and academic progression

Promotion to the next stage of the programme is governed by the credit requirements in R-26. For B.Tech., the regulations specify minimum earned-credit conditions for progression from First Year to Second Year, Second Year to Third Year, and Third Year to Final Year. Degree award requires completion of the prescribed programme credits and all mandatory non-credit courses, along with other applicable academic and administrative requirements. M.Tech. students must satisfy the prescribed credit and mandatory course requirements for the degree.

Students should refer to the full R-26 regulations and their programme-specific approved curriculum for the exact progression conditions applicable to their admission route.
