ASSESSMENT & OBE

Rubric Manual 2026–27

ADYPSOE – Rubric Manual for Outcome-Based Assessment of Student Performance (Academic Year 2026–27).

Reference document for faculty on the design and application of analytic rubrics across assessment tools under the OBE framework. It includes the common four-level analytic scale, guidelines for use, Bloom’s Taxonomy-to-assessment-tool mapping, ten assessment rubrics, score computation and a summary annexure.

The manual is issued by the Accreditation and OBE Cell with the approval of the Internal Quality Assurance Cell.

Bloom’s Taxonomy – Question-Framing Chart

OBE question paper setting reference for engineering courses, covering revised taxonomy levels L1–L6, cognitive focus, action verbs and question stems/examples. Includes a reminder to classify the complete question by the thinking required, rather than relying on the verb alone.
