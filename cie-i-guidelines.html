MidSem Examination Guidelines

Guidance on the CIE-I MidSem examination component for autonomous courses at ADYPSOE.

CIE-I · 16 marks towards CIE

Scope and weightage

CIE-I is the MidSem Examination component of Continuous Internal Evaluation. It is based on Units 1 and 2 and is conducted for a raw weightage of 20 marks. The marks obtained are subsequently converted to 16 marks for the CIE scheme.

Converted CIE-I Marks = (Marks obtained out of 20 ÷ 20) × 16
Equivalent calculation: Marks obtained × 0.8

Example: If a student obtains 15 out of 20, the converted CIE-I score is 15 × 0.8 = 12 out of 16.

ASSESSMENT SCOPE

Coverage of the examination

  • The examination assesses the prescribed content of Unit 1 and Unit 2 of the applicable course syllabus.
  • Faculty should ensure that the questions are within the syllabus coverage communicated to students for the MidSem examination.
  • Questions should assess the intended Course Outcomes (COs) and the level of learning expected from the covered units.
  • Any course-specific clarification or examination instruction must be communicated through the authorised institute/department channel.

OBE ALIGNMENT

Course Outcome mapping and Bloom’s Taxonomy

Question setters should identify the CO(s) assessed by each question or sub-question and indicate the relevant cognitive level using the revised Bloom’s Taxonomy.

LevelCognitive processIndicative action verbs
L1RememberDefine, state, list, identify, recall
L2UnderstandExplain, describe, summarize, interpret
L3ApplyApply, solve, calculate, demonstrate
L4AnalyzeAnalyze, differentiate, examine, investigate
L5EvaluateEvaluate, justify, critique, assess
L6CreateDesign, develop, formulate, construct

The cognitive level must reflect the thinking required to answer the complete question. The action verb alone does not determine the Bloom’s level.

FACULTY RESPONSIBILITIES

Question paper preparation and evaluation

  • Prepare questions strictly from Units 1 and 2 of the applicable approved syllabus.
  • Ensure that questions are clear, technically correct, unambiguous and appropriate to the expected student level.
  • Map questions to the relevant COs and Bloom’s Taxonomy levels in the prescribed question paper format, wherever required.
  • Verify marks, sub-question totals, internal choices (if prescribed), instructions and page/figure references before submission.
  • Maintain confidentiality of question papers and follow the Examination Section’s notified submission and moderation process.
  • Evaluate answer scripts consistently against the approved marking scheme and submit marks within the notified timeline.

STUDENT GUIDANCE

Preparation and examination conduct

  • Prepare the topics covered in Units 1 and 2 as per the course syllabus and instructions issued by the faculty.
  • Check the official examination timetable, venue and reporting instructions before the examination.
  • Carry the required hall ticket/identity document and follow the instructions of the invigilators.
  • Read each question carefully and observe the marks indicated against questions and sub-questions.
  • Follow the institute’s examination rules and avoid any unauthorised materials or devices.
