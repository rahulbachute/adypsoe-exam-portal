Student-facing overview of the ADYPSOE Rules and Regulations for Examination and Evaluation, R-26 / Version 1.0, effective from Academic Year 2026–27.

Official reference · R-26

Read the approved manual

This webpage is a concise guide to selected provisions of the Examination Manual. It does not replace the complete approved Rules and Regulations. In case of any difference, omission or interpretation issue, refer to the approved manual and the competent statutory authority.

Applicability: The manual states that these regulations apply to B.Tech. and M.Tech. students admitted with effect from AY 2026–27. Since autonomy is being implemented progressively, students must verify the applicable programme, admission batch and semester before relying on an autonomous provision.

All three requirements must be satisfied to pass a theory course. The manual states that there is no separate passing requirement for MSE and CCE individually; they are combined for the CIE requirement.

Practical / Term Work / Oral: The manual prescribes a minimum of 40% in each respective evaluation head, subject to the approved course scheme.

ATTENDANCE AND ELIGIBILITY

3. Attendance and examination eligibility

  • Minimum attendance prescribed: 75% in each course, subject to applicable approved condonation provisions.
  • A student who does not meet the attendance requirement may be detained in the course and may not be permitted to appear for the relevant examination.
  • Attendance status is to be communicated periodically, with an opportunity for students to report discrepancies within the notified period.
  • Examination forms must be submitted with the prescribed fee within the notified schedule. Late submission, where permitted, is subject to the prescribed late fee and deadline.
  • A detained student is not permitted to fill the examination form for the relevant examination.

RE-EXAMINATION

4. Re-SEE and Summer / Backlog Examination

  • Re-SEE is for eligible students who have failed the SEE (F grade) or have an approved IC arising from absence in SEE.
  • CIE marks already earned are carried forward; CIE is not re-conducted as part of Re-SEE.
  • A student with FX due to attendance or another course deficiency is not eligible for Re-SEE until the deficiency is resolved through the applicable re-registration/course-completion process.
  • Re-SEE is not a grade-improvement examination. The prescribed examination form and fee must be submitted within the notified schedule.
  • Summer / Backlog examinations are conducted as notified, subject to eligibility, registration, course offering and applicable rules.

ACADEMIC PROGRESSION

6. Promotion and ATKT

  • For promotion from First Year to Second Year, a student must earn at least 50% of the total credits of First Year.
  • For promotion from Second Year to Third Year, the student must earn at least 50% of Second Year credits and all First Year credits.
  • For promotion from Third Year to Final Year, the student must earn at least 50% of Third Year credits and all Second Year credits.
  • Degree award is subject to earning the prescribed programme credits and clearing all mandatory non-credit courses and other applicable requirements.

Refer to the complete manual for the detailed B.Tech. and M.Tech. progression conditions and any applicable programme-specific provisions.

RESULTS AND STUDENT REPRESENTATION

7. Results, answer-book queries and grievances

  • Results are declared by the Controller of Examinations after the prescribed examination and result-approval process.
  • The manual provides for an Open House after subjective-mode SEE assessment. Students with queries may submit them in the prescribed format during the notified Open House.
  • Only candidates appearing for the Open House are permitted under the manual to apply for grievances relating to that process.
  • Challenges to results and handling/inspection of evaluated answer scripts are governed by the approved examination and grievance procedure.
  • Revaluation is not permitted for Practical, Oral, Term Work or CIE components unless specifically provided otherwise under applicable regulations.

EXAMINATION CONDUCT

8. Important instructions for students

  • Be present outside the examination hall at least 20 minutes before the examination starts.
  • Carry the institute identity card and hall ticket; identity verification is required for entry.
  • Do not communicate with other students or outsiders during the examination.
  • Mobile phones, smart watches, electronic devices and unauthorised written/electronic material are prohibited.
  • Only non-programmable scientific calculators are permitted unless a different requirement is specifically approved or notified.
  • Submit every answer book, including a blank answer book, to the invigilator before leaving.
  • Follow invigilator instructions and the notified rules for entry, exit, washroom breaks and examination materials.

MALPRACTICE AND DISCIPLINE

9. Unfair means and examination misconduct

Reported cases are dealt with under the approved Unfair Means / Malpractice procedure. The Lapses, Malpractice and Examination Complaint Redressal Committee (LMECRC) inquires into reported cases, examines evidence and provides the concerned person an opportunity to be heard. It submits findings and recommendations to the competent authority for a final decision.

For students and faculty

10. Important interpretation note

This page summarises selected provisions from the uploaded R-26 manual and is not an amendment or independent interpretation. The manual contains provisions that require confirmation or harmonisation before they are reproduced as definitive public instructions—for example, the unit-wise allocation for the 20-mark Mid-Sem examination appears differently in different passages. Therefore, this page does not state a unit-wise raw-mark split. The approved examination scheme, official circulars and decisions of the competent authority prevail.

Students should always check the latest official notice for the relevant programme, semester, admission batch and examination session.
